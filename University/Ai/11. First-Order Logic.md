Extends propositional logic in 2 ways:
- meanings of 'atomic' propositions may be represented in terms of properties and relations holding among named objects
- expressive power is further increased by the use of variables and 'quantifiers' which can be used to represent generalised or non-specific information
Quantifier called first-order if its  variable ranges over individual entities of a domain. Second-order quantification is where the quantified variable ranges over sets of entities. In this course we shall restrict our attention to the first-order case
